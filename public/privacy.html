One Thing privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Short version: One Thing has no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Everything you type, and everything it counts about your phone use, stays on your phone. We cannot see it.

What the app keeps, and where

Your plans, tasks, goals, ideas, decisions, settings and your focus picture are saved only on your phone, inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app or clear its data, they are gone.

Backup file. When you tap Export backup, the app makes a file and you choose where it goes (for example your Downloads or Google Drive). That place is your choice and follows that service's rules.

Phone time and the scroll bubble (Android app)

If you switch on Usage access, One Thing reads Android's own record of which app was open and for how long. It uses this to show your phone time, to count your scrolling minutes, and for the focus lock. It reads app names and minutes only, never what you see or type inside other apps. The numbers stay on your phone.

If you switch on Display over other apps, One Thing can show the small scroll bubble on top of the apps you picked, and bring you back to the app during a focus session.

You can switch both off at any time in your phone settings.

Microphone

The microphone is used only after you tap the mic on the Talk screen, and only while it listens. Your phone's speech service (in Chrome: the browser's speech service) turns your words into text. That service may be run by Google and may need the internet; it follows its own privacy policy. One Thing does not record, keep or send the audio itself. Only the text is used, on your phone.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are set with your phone's own alarm system. In the web version, a reminder opens Google Calendar with the text filled in, and you decide whether to save it there.

What we never do

  • No sign-up and no account.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • No sharing of your data with anyone.

Children

One Thing is not made for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
